Research

MANARA Lab · College of Computing · UM6P

Research Interests

  • Trustworthy & Explainable AI: Explainable AI (XAI), trust and fairness in AI systems, blockchain-based and cross-domain recommender systems, authorship verification and stylometry.
  • Wireless Networks & Networked Systems: 5G/6G Communications, NOMA, IRS-assisted communications, Internet of Things (IoT), resource management in wireless networks.
  • Security, Privacy & Blockchain: Physical layer security, trust and reputation management, federated learning with privacy guarantees, cloud forensics, decentralized systems.
  • Speech & Affective Computing: Speaker identification, speech emotion recognition, overlapping speech detection, multimodal audio-visual processing.
  • Healthcare AI & Medical Imaging: AI-driven clinical decision support, cardiovascular risk prediction, medical image analysis.
  • Mobile Crowdsensing: Task allocation, location privacy, crowdsensing frameworks.
