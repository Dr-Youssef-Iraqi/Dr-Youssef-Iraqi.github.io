# dr-youssef-iraqi.github.io

Personal academic webpage of Prof. Youssef Iraqi, Associate Professor at the
College of Computing, University Mohammed VI Polytechnic (UM6P), Benguerir, Morocco.

🌐 Live site: https://dr-youssef-iraqi.github.io

## Structure

- `index.html`: Home (news, bio, honors and awards, contact)
- `research.html`: Research interests, selected publications, funded projects, and patents
- `publications.html`: Full publication list (journals, conferences, book chapters, abstracts)
- `team.html`: Ph.D. students, postdoctoral fellows, pre-doctoral students, and alumni
- `teaching.html`: Teaching history
- `service.html`: Editorial boards, invited addresses, conference organizing, and program committees
- `software.html`: Software and dataset releases
- `style.css`: Shared stylesheet

## Content

The content follows my CV (November 2026). The publication list, team, and funded projects
are generated from the LaTeX source of the CV, so the site and the CV use the same entries
and numbering. Supervised students and postdoctoral fellows are underlined in author lists.
