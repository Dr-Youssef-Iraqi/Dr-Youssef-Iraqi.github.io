About

Youssef Iraqi received the M.Sc. and Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Montreal, Canada, in 2000 and 2003, respectively, and the Engineering Degree in Computer Engineering (First Class Honors) from ENSIAS, Mohammed V University, Rabat, Morocco, in 1995. He is currently an Associate Professor at the College of Computing, University Mohammed VI Polytechnic (UM6P), Morocco, where he leads the MANARA Lab (Multimodal AI, Networks, Analytics, Recommender Systems & Augmented Sensing Laboratory).

Before joining UM6P in 2021, he was with the Electrical and Computer Engineering Department at Khalifa University, United Arab Emirates, from 2009 to 2021. From 2005 to 2009, he was with the Department of Computer Science at Dhofar University, Oman, which he chaired from 2006 to 2009. From 2004 to 2005, he was a Research Assistant Professor at the School of Computer Science, University of Waterloo, Canada, after a postdoctoral fellowship there.

He has published more than 170 research papers in international journals and refereed conference proceedings, and holds 5 US patents, with one further US patent application filed in 2026. His research spans trustworthy and explainable AI, wireless networks and networked systems, security, privacy and blockchain, speech and affective computing, healthcare AI and medical imaging, and mobile crowdsensing. In 2008, he received the IEEE Communications Society Fred W. Ellersick Prize Paper Award in the Field of Communications Systems.
